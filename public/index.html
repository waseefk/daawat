About us

We have made delicious food since 1990

For 36 years, we have catered with one promise: to make your special occasion delicious and memorable.

We are specialist biryani makers and caterers in Chennai. Our biryani adds a special note to any party, and its rich taste gives your guests a tongue-tingling experience. Our customers' feedback has been excellent, and we listen carefully to every suggestion so that Daawat stays one of the best biryani makers and caterers in Chennai.

We take orders for mutton and chicken biryani from all parts of Chennai, for general, party and wedding orders, at competitive prices and without compromising on quality or hygiene. The minimum order is 1 kg, and we deliver free of charge for catering orders of 10 kg and above.

Our special menu

Home-style Indian food, cooked the traditional way

Every dish is prepared using the traditional cooking methods that are unique to it. Our team of chefs comes from across North and South India, and each is an expert in their own cuisine.

Chicken Biryani

A savoury rice dish loaded with spicy marinated chicken, caramelised onions, saffron, spices and herbs, cooked over low heat for the deepest flavour.

Mutton Biryani

Long-grain basmati rice layered with tender mutton marinated in rich spices and herbs, slow-cooked in a copper handi.

Hyderabadi Dum Biryani

The world-famous biryani of Hyderabad: meat and rice sealed and cooked together on dum. A complete meal in itself.

Veg Biryani

Fresh vegetables marinated in spices and yoghurt, infused with saffron and desi ghee, and slowly cooked between layers of basmati rice in handis.

Raita

Our cool, creamy raita makes every biryani more delightful and brings out its flavours.

Brinjal Chutney

A spicy, tangy tamarind gravy made with brinjal (baingan), the classic partner to biryani.

Chicken Kebab

Succulent pieces of deep-fried chicken with a crispy, flavour-packed coating. A must try.

Mutton Gravy

Fresh, tender mutton cooked with tamarind and freshly ground spices. Perfect with biryani.

Gulab Jamun

Soft, spongy, melt-in-the-mouth jamuns soaked in delicately flavoured sugar syrup. A traditional Indian sweet.

Phirni

A chilled, creamy pudding of ground rice and milk, also known as firni, from the cuisine of the Indian subcontinent.

Bread Halwa

A rich, mouth-watering sweet made from soaked bread, ghee and condensed milk.

Kesar Pista Kheer

A simple, classic rice kheer with a fine blend of saffron and pistachios.

Also on request: Fish Biryani, Chicken Gravy, Prawn Masala and more. Call us to plan your menu.
